# Which video model for which job (course notes, Sep 2026)

Model advice goes out of date quickly. Treat as guidance, not fact.

When choosing an AI video model, don’t think, “Which one is best?”
Think, “What am I trying to make?”
Seedance 2.0 is by far the best all round model right now, but it is also the most expensive. If you have the budget and want the best results, it's quite far ahead of the rest.
On Seedance you can upload up to 9 image, 3 videos & 3 Audio as references, at the same time.

Kling 3.0 4k is great for B-roll and slower paced Cinematic shots, and if you need to control the direction of the movement more precisely, use Kling Motion Control.
Google Gemini Omni is great for lip-sync and video editing. Use this as a back-up to Seedance when you want great lip-sync.
Veo 3.1 is good for cheaper lip-sync and scenes, but is not on the same level as Seedance or Gemini Omni.
Veo 3.1 has 2 settings, Quality or Fast. Quality costs more credits but gives a slightly better output.
Heygen is the best at creating clones and doing 'Talking Head' Videos. Look at Phase 9 for info on cloning yourself.
For quick tests, use cheaper or faster models. Once the idea works, regenerate the best version with a higher-quality model.

## The simple rule is this:
• Overall best for Avatars - Seedance 2.0
• Overall best for B-roll/Cinematic slow shots - Kling 3.0
• Overall great at most things - Google Gemini Omni
• Overall best for Cloning & Talking head - Heygen

## Lip-sync test findings (AI Lipsync Test lesson)

Same character, framing and script across every model. Results at the time of recording:

* **Seedance:** by far the best. The face feels alive, the mouth is cleaner, expressions connect to the sentence, and the character doesn't feel like a still image forced to talk. Use it for the hero version / the one you show a client.
* **Gemini Omni:** second choice, ahead of Veo. Consistent lip-sync across settings and lighting (even harsh sun) with basic prompts, and a much more natural, non-robotic voice. **Workflow:** make cheap versions in Gemini Omni to find the best hook, then recreate the winning direction in Seedance.
* **Veo 3.1 Fast / Quality:** strong but only 8 seconds per clip, so shorten the script. Small mouth glitches; voice needs work. Quality is only very slightly better than Fast; the instructor says the price difference isn't worth it. Music and editing hide a lot.
* **Kling 3.0 / Kling Omni:** fine for B-roll and cinematic clips, weak for talking avatars. **Past about 10 seconds the lip-sync loses timing and "goes to mush"**, and voices sound robotic. If you must use it, keep speech under ~10s.
* **Grok Imagine:** very clean lip-sync but a robotic, voiceover-like voice.

**Lip-sync prompts don't need to be complicated.** The test prompt was simply: `She is vlogging, she says, "Let me tell you a secret, you can make avatars like me to promote your products or start social channels. When you think about it, the possibilities really are endless when you use AI."` The quality comes from a good start frame, a natural advert-style script and the right model. Watch for: mouth slightly late, strange teeth, face drifting, "miming" instead of speaking.
