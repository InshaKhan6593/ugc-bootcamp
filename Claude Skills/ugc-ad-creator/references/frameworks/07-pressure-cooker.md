# Framework 7: The Pressure Cooker

Psychology, step breakdown, script template, example script and hook bank.

*Problem, Tension, Relief*

### The Psychology

This is one of the oldest and most effective persuasion structures in human communication. You identify a wound, press on it until the viewer can feel the pain, then offer the relief. The neurological effect is powerful. The brain experiences genuine tension during the "agitate" phase and genuine relief during the solution, which creates a strong positive association with your product.

**Quick Reference: W\.O.U.N.D.**

W = Where It Hurts
O = Open the Wound
U = Understand Their Failures
N = Name the New Way
D = Deliver the Proof + CTA

### DETAILED BREAKDOWN

### W Where It Hurts

Identify the core pain point. Not a feature gap, an emotional wound. How does the problem make them FEEL? Frustrated? Embarrassed? Hopeless? Scared?

**Example:**

*"That moment when you can't zip up the jeans that fit you 6 months ago"*

*"Lying awake at 3am because your mind won't stop racing about tomorrow"*

### O Open the Wound

Go deeper. Show them the ripple effects of this problem. How it affects their relationships, confidence, career, health. Stack specific scenarios.

**Example:**

*"It starts with skipping the pool party. Then avoiding the beach trip. Then you stop looking in mirrors. Then you're ordering food for the clothing size, not the flavor."*

## U Understand Their Failures

Acknowledge what they've already tried. This is critical. It shows empathy and positions your product as different from all the things that already disappointed them.

**Example:**

*"You've tried the cleanses. The fad diets. The $200 programs. Maybe they worked for a week. Maybe they didn't work at all."*

## N Name the New Way

Introduce your product not as another thing to try, but as a fundamentally different approach. Explain the mechanism, why THIS works when other things haven't.

**Example:**

*"What if the problem was never willpower? What if it was your gut biome? [Product] works at the source, [specific mechanism], so your body stops fighting you."*

## D Deliver the Proof + CTA

Combine social proof with a CTA that offers risk reversal. The viewer is emotionally primed, so reduce the friction to zero.

**Example:**

*"47,000 people already feel the difference. 90 day money back guarantee. Your body deserves better."*

**SCRIPT TEMPLATE**

HOOK: [Where It Hurts]

*"[Vivid, specific moment that captures the emotional core of the problem]"*

BODY:

[Open the Wound] *"And it doesn't stop there. [Ripple effect 1]. [Ripple effect 2]. Pretty soon you're [worst case emotional scenario]."*

[Understand Their Failures] *"I know because I tried everything. [Failed solution 1]. [Failed solution 2]. [How it felt when those failed]."*

[Name the New Way] *"Then I learned the real problem was [root cause]. [Product] addresses that directly by [mechanism]."*

CTA: [Deliver the Proof + CTA]

*"[Social proof stat]. [Risk reversal offer]. [Emotional benefit]."*

"That feeling when you've been staring at the same paragraph for 20 minutes and nothing is going in. Your brain just... isn't there."

"And it doesn't just stay at work. You go home and your partner is telling you about their day but you're already somewhere else. You can't finish a book. [Holds up ClearMind bottle]

"Then my doctor explained something that changed everything. The issue isn't focus. It's neuroinflammation. When your brain is chronically inflamed from stress, poor sleep, and screen time, your neurons can't communicate efficiently. It's like trying to have a phone call with static on the line. ClearMind targets that inflammation directly. Lion's mane for nerve growth factor. Omega-3 DHA for membrane fluidity. Bacopa for neurotransmitter balance. It's not a stimulant. It's repair."

## CTA (42-55 seconds):

"28,000 people are thinking clearer right now because they stopped trying to caffeinate their way through brain fog. 90-day money back guarantee, no questions asked. If your brain doesn't feel sharper in 30 days, you pay nothing. Your brain deserves better than another cup of coffee."
