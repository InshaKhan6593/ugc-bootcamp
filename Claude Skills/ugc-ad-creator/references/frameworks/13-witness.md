# Framework 13: The Witness

Psychology, step breakdown, script template, example script and hook bank.

*Testimonial Cascade*

### The Psychology

Third party proof is exponentially more persuasive than first party claims. When someone who has nothing to gain says "this changed my life," it carries 10x the weight of the brand saying the same thing. A cascade of testimonials creates a "social proof avalanche" where each new voice makes the product seem more inevitable.

### Quick Reference: V.O.I.C.E.

V = Verify the Pain

O = Open the Discovery

I = Illustrate the Change

C = Cascade the Social Proof

E = Endorse and Encourage

### DETAILED BREAKDOWN

### V Verify the Pain

Open with a real customer describing the specific problem they had. This should sound raw and conversational, not scripted.

**Example:**

*"I was spending $200 a month on skincare and my face still looked like a pizza"*

### O Open the Discovery

The customer describes how they found the product. Casual, organic, not a pitch.

**Example:**

*"My coworker had insanely clear skin and I finally asked her. She just showed me this one thing."*

### I Illustrate the Change

Walk through the transformation timeline. Specific details and sensory observations are key.

**Example:**

"Two weeks in, my forehead cleared up. By week four, people were asking what I changed. Month two, I wore no foundation for the first time since high school."

## C Cascade the Social Proof

Stack multiple testimonials. Quick cuts. Different people, different demographics, all saying variations of the same thing. Volume equals credibility.

**Example:**

"Game changer, honestly"

"I threw out my entire old routine"

"My dermatologist asked me what I was using"

"Bought one for my mom, she's obsessed"

## E Endorse and Encourage

The original testimonial giver returns for the CTA. It's their recommendation, not the brand's.

**Example:**

"If you've tried everything and nothing works, this was me. Just try it. Worst case you return it. Best case you stop buying 10 products."

## SCRIPT TEMPLATE

HOOK: [Verify the Pain]

"[Customer voice]: I was [relatable struggle, be specific and honest]."

BODY:

[Open the Discovery] "[Casual discovery story, friend recommended, saw it online, etc.]"

[Illustrate the Change] "[Week 1 experience]. [Week 2 to 3, first noticeable change]. [Month 1 to 2, significant transformation with specific detail]."

[Cascade the Social Proof, 4 to 6 quick cuts]

"[Short testimonial 1]" / "[Short testimonial 2]" / "[Short testimonial 3]" / "[Short testimonial 4]"

CTA: [Endorse and Encourage]

"[Original customer returns]: If you're where I was, just try it. [Risk reversal]. [Honest recommendation]."

## EXAMPLE FULL SCRIPT

**Product**: GutReset Probiotic

### HOOK (0-5 seconds):

[Woman, mid-30s, sitting casually on a couch, talking to camera]

"I was spending $300 a month on digestive supplements and I still couldn't eat a meal without looking 6 months pregnant by dessert."

## DISCOVERY (5-15 seconds):

"My sister, who never recommends anything, texted me a link and just said 'try this, trust me.' She'd been dealing with the same bloating for years and I noticed on Thanksgiving she ate two plates and didn't complain once. That's when I knew something was different."

## CHANGE (15-30 seconds):

"First week on GutReset, honestly? Nothing dramatic. I almost gave up. But by day 10 I noticed I wasn't unbuttoning my jeans after lunch anymore. Week three, I ate pasta for dinner and woke up with a flat stomach. That hadn't happened in 5 years. By month two, I went to brunch with friends, ate pancakes, eggs, bacon, the works, and felt completely fine after. My friend literally said 'are you on something?' and I showed her the bottle."

## CASCADE (30-42 seconds):

[Quick cuts, different people, different backgrounds]

"I can eat bread again without paying for it later."

"My bloating went from every single day to maybe once a week."

"I threw out everything else. This is the only thing that worked."

"My gastroenterologist asked what I changed. She wrote it down."

"I bought 6 bottles for Christmas gifts. Not even kidding."

## CTA (42-55 seconds):

[Original woman comes back on screen]

"If you're where I was, bloated every day, afraid to eat the foods you love, spending money on stuff that doesn't work, just try it. They have a 90-day money-back guarantee so there's literally no risk. Worst case you return it. Best case you stop dreading meals."

## HOOK BANK

1. "I was the biggest skeptic. [X] weeks later I'm buying it for everyone I know."

2. "When [X] different people tell you the same thing... maybe listen"

3. "My [friend/family member] told me to try this. I thought they were crazy."

4. "Real talk: I almost returned this. Then week [X] happened."

5. "I've never been the person to recommend products, but..."

6. "Here's what [X] people said after trying [Product]:"

7. "I asked 50 customers to be honest about [Product]. Here's what they said."

8. "Before you buy [category], watch what actual users think"

9. "Don't take my word for it. Take theirs."

10. "The reviews were too good to be true. So I tried it myself. They were right."
