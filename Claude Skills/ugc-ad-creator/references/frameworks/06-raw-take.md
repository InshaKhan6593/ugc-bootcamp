# Framework 6: The Raw Take

Psychology, step breakdown, script template, example script and hook bank.

*Organic / Native Style*

### The Psychology

As consumers become more ad literate, they develop what psychologists call "ad blindness." Anything that looks or feels like an ad gets scrolled past instantly. The Raw Take exploits this by disguising your ad as native content, something that looks like it belongs in the viewer's regular feed. The moment it stops feeling like an ad, watch time skyrockets.

### Quick Reference: B.L.E.N.D.

B = Begin Like Content
L = Lead with a Relatable Moment
E = Ease in the Product
N = Narrate the Experience
D = Deliver a Soft Close

### DETAILED BREAKDOWN

### B Begin Like Content

Start exactly like a regular TikTok, Reel, or YouTube short. No brand mentions. No product in frame. Just a person sharing something interesting. The first 3 seconds should be indistinguishable from organic content.

**Example:**

"Okay so I literally just discovered something and I need to tell someone" (selfie style, slightly rushed, imperfect framing)

### L Lead with a Relatable Moment

Share a personal story or observation that your target audience will deeply relate to. This builds connection before any selling happens.

**Example:**

"I've been trying to get my energy back after having my second kid and honestly nothing was working. I've tried every supplement, every routine, every hack..."

## E Ease in the Product

The product mention should feel incidental, not planned. Like they're just sharing what worked for them personally. Use phrases like "so I tried," "someone told me about," or "I randomly found."

**Example:**

"...then my sister literally handed me this thing and was like 'just try it.' I was skeptical but whatever."

## N Narrate the Experience

Describe the actual experience of using the product. Sensory details, timeline, honest reactions including initial skepticism. Authenticity is everything.

**Example:**

"First week? Nothing. Honestly I almost stopped. But by week three I noticed I wasn't crashing at 2pm anymore. By week six my husband was like 'what happened to you?'"

## D Deliver a Soft Close

The CTA should feel like a friend's recommendation, not a pitch. No urgency tactics. No scarcity. Just "here's where to get it if you want to try."

**Example:**

"Anyway I just wanted to share in case anyone else is going through the same thing. I'll put the link up if you want to check it out."

## SCRIPT TEMPLATE

**HOOK**: [Begin Like Content]

"Okay so [casual opening that sounds like regular content, no product mention]"

BODY:

[Lead with a Relatable Moment] "I've been dealing with [relatable problem] for [time period]. I tried [thing 1], [thing 2], honestly [thing 3]... nothing."

[Ease in the Product] "Then [casual discovery method]. I was honestly skeptical but [reason they tried it anyway]."

[Narrate the Experience] "[Honest timeline]. [Week 1 reaction]. [Week X, when they noticed something]. [Specific change]."

**CTA**: [Deliver a Soft Close]

"Anyway, just wanted to share in case it helps someone. [Casual link mention]."

## EXAMPLE FULL SCRIPT

**Product**: DreamDrop Sleep Supplement

**HOOK (0-5 seconds):**

[Selfie camera, person in bed with messy hair, no makeup, talking casually like they just thought of this]
"Okay wait, I need to tell someone this because I'm genuinely freaking out a little bit."

**RELATABLE MOMENT (5-18 seconds):**

"So I've been the worst sleeper my entire adult life. I'm talking lying in bed for two hours, brain going a million miles an hour, finally falling asleep at 1am, then waking up at 4am for no reason and just... staring at the ceiling. I tried melatonin, made me groggy. Tried magnesium, did nothing. Tried those expensive sleep drinks, they just made me pee at 3am. I've been running on like 5 hours of broken sleep for literally years."

**EASE IN (18-28 seconds):**

"My roommate left this on the kitchen counter like two weeks ago and I honestly thought it was another one of her wellness fads. She takes like 30 supplements. But she's been sleeping like a rock and I've been jealous, so I was like whatever, I'll try it."

[Casually holds up DreamDrop bottle, not in a posed way]

**EXPERIENCE (28-45 seconds):**

"First night? I noticed my brain got quieter about 30 minutes after taking it. Like not drowsy, just... calm. I fell asleep in maybe 20 minutes instead of my usual 90. I figured it was placebo. Second night, same thing. By day 5 I slept 7 straight hours for the first time in I don't even know how long. Last night I slept 8 hours and I woke up before my alarm. I didn't even know that was possible for me. My roommate was like 'told you' and honestly I'm annoyed she was right."

**SOFT CLOSE (45-55 seconds):**

"Anyway I just wanted to share because I know there are people like me who've tried everything and given up. I almost didn't try this because I was so over sleep supplements. Glad I did. I'll put the link up if you want to check it out. No pressure, obviously."

**HOOK BANK**

1. "Okay wait I need to talk about something" (selfie style, mid activity)

2. "I've been keeping this to myself but I feel like I need to share"

3. "Someone asked me what changed and honestly it's kind of embarrassing"

4. "This is random but I just realized something about [category]"

5. "POV: you accidentally find something that actually works"

6. "Not sponsored, not an ad, just genuinely obsessed with this thing"

7. "I wasn't going to post this but too many people are asking"

8. "Can we talk about [topic] for a second? Because I have thoughts."

9. "Okay storytime, because this genuinely surprised me"

10. "I don't usually recommend things but I can't keep quiet about this"
