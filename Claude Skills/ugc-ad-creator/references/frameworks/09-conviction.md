# Framework 9: The Conviction

Psychology, step breakdown, script template, example script and hook bank.

*Founder Objection Crusher*

**The Psychology**

Every potential buyer has a voice in their head saying "but what about...?" The most effective sales technique isn't to ignore objections, it's to bring them up FIRST, louder and more bluntly than the customer would, then demolish them. When a founder pre-addresses doubt, it signals radical confidence and builds extraordinary trust.

**Quick Reference: S.T.E.E.L.**

S = State the Harsh Truth
T = Tell Your Identity
E = Echo the Exact Doubt
E = Eliminate with Evidence
L = Launch the Bigger Play

**DETAILED BREAKDOWN**

### S State the Harsh Truth

Open with an uncomfortable reality about your market. Something undeniably true that immediately establishes credibility because most brands would never say it.

**Example:**

*"90% of supplements on Amazon are glorified sugar pills. I know because I used to manufacture them."*

### T Tell Your Identity

Establish why YOU specifically have the credibility to challenge the status quo. This isn't bragging, it's positioning.

**Example:**

*"But we're not marketers who hired scientists. We're scientists who learned marketing."*

### E Echo the Exact Doubt

Say the objection out loud. Quote the skeptics. Use the exact language a doubter would use. This shows you understand their resistance.

**Example:**

*"People said: 'You'll never compete with the big brands.' 'Your packaging looks cheap.' 'Nobody cares about clean ingredients.'"*

## E Eliminate with Evidence

Now crush each objection with specific, verifiable proof. Match each doubt with an undeniable win.

**Example:**

*"In 8 months: outsold [Competitor] in our category. Named 'Best New Brand' by [Publication]. 12,000 five star reviews."*

## L Launch the Bigger Play

Expand the vision beyond today's product. Show that this is a movement, not a moment.

**Example:**

*"Next year we're open sourcing our formulas. If the big brands won't be transparent, we'll force the entire industry to be."*

## EXAMPLE FULL SCRIPT

**Product**: PureRoots Organic Skincare

### HOOK (0-5 seconds):

[Founder in a lab coat, standing in a small production facility, talking directly to camera]

"I'm going to tell you something that might make you uncomfortable. 80% of 'organic' skincare brands use the exact same base formula from the same three manufacturers in China. They just change the label."

### IDENTITY (5-15 seconds):

"My name is Dr. Amara Osei. I'm a cosmetic chemist. I spent 9 years formulating products for brands you've definitely heard of. And I left because I couldn't stomach watching companies charge $80 for a serum that costs $1.20 to make. We're not influencers who launched a skincare line. We're chemists who got tired of the lies."

### DOUBTS (15-28 seconds):

"When we launched PureRoots, the internet had opinions. 'Your packaging looks like it was designed in Microsoft Word.' Fair. 'Nobody is going to pay $35 for a serum from a brand they've never heard of.' Heard that a lot. 'You can't compete with Drunk Elephant and The Ordinary.' That was my favorite one. And the best: 'Clean skincare doesn't work as well as the real stuff.' As if chemicals are the only way to get results."

### EVIDENCE (28-42 seconds):

"Here's what actually happened. 14 months in: we outsold two of those 'unbeatable' brands in our category on Amazon. 9,400 five-star reviews, and 73% of reviewers specifically mention visible results within 3 weeks. A blind clinical study by an independent lab showed our Vitamin C serum outperformed Drunk Elephant's by 22% in collagen production. Our 'Microsoft Word packaging' brand just got featured in Vogue, Allure, and the New York Times. Same month."

### BIGGER PLAY + CTA (42-55 seconds):

"And we're just getting started. Next quarter we're publishing every single formula on our website. Full transparency. Every ingredient, every percentage, every supplier. If the big brands won't show you what's in the bottle, we'll show you what's in ours and let you decide. If you want skincare made by actual scientists instead of marketing departments, the link is below."

**HOOK BANK**

1. "They said [specific objection]. They were wrong. Here's the proof."

2. "The biggest lie in [industry], and how we proved it wrong"

3. "Everyone told us this was impossible. [X] months later..."

4. "I'll say what no one in [industry] will: [harsh truth]"

5. "Here's why [common objection] is completely backwards"

6. "We got laughed out of [X]. Then we [undeniable result]."

7. "The [industry] called us crazy. Then they called us competition."

8. "They said we'd fail in 6 months. We hit [metric] in 3."

9. "If you think [category] can't be disrupted, watch this."

10. "Nobody believed a [small detail] could beat [big competitor]. The numbers say otherwise."
