# Framework 4: The Origin Story

Psychology, step breakdown, script template, example script and hook bank.

*Founder Led Narrative*

### The Psychology

People don't buy products, they buy into people. When a founder tells their story authentically, it activates narrative transportation: the viewer's brain literally simulates the experience as if it were happening to them. This creates emotional bonds that no amount of feature listing can match. Origin stories also make the brand feel human, mission driven, and trustworthy.

### Quick Reference: B.U.I.L.T.

B = Begin with the Human
U = Uncover the Enemy
I = Ignition Moment
L = Lay Out the Wins
T = Tomorrow's Vision

## DETAILED BREAKDOWN

### B Begin with the Human

Introduce the founder as a real person, not a CEO. Show them in a context that makes them relatable. Vulnerability is currency here.

**Example:**

*"This is James. Two years ago he was sleeping in his car between shifts."*

### U Uncover the Enemy

Every good origin story has an antagonist. This isn't a competitor, it's a systemic problem that affects your ideal customer. The founder didn't just see this problem, they LIVED it.

**Example:**

*"He watched his mother spend $400/month on medications that barely worked, and no one seemed to care."*

### I Ignition Moment

The breakthrough. The "what if" moment. This is the emotional peak of the story, the point where the founder decided to do something about it.

**Example:**

*"One night he thought: what if the delivery system was the problem, not the ingredients?"*

## L Lay Out the Wins

Show measurable traction. Revenue, users, impact metrics, partnerships. Use 3 to 4 data points that demonstrate this isn't just an idea, it's working.

**Example:**

*"18 months later: 50,000 customers. 4.9 star rating. Featured in Forbes. Profitable from day one."*

## T Tomorrow's Vision

Connect today's success to a bigger mission. Make it clear this is chapter one, not the whole book. This gives viewers something to root for.

**Example:**

*"But this is just the beginning. By 2027, we're bringing this to 12 countries where people are paying 10x more for medicine that works half as well."*

### SCRIPT TEMPLATE

HOOK: [Begin with the Human]
*"This is [Name]. [Relatable, humanizing detail]."*

BODY:
[Uncover the Enemy] *"[He/She/They] saw [systemic problem] firsthand. [Emotional specific detail]."*
[Ignition Moment] *"Then came the moment everything changed: [the insight/breakthrough]."*
[Lay Out the Wins] *"[Time period] later: [Metric 1]. [Metric 2]. [Metric 3]. [Metric 4]."*
[Tomorrow's Vision] *"But [Name]'s not done. [Bigger mission that aligns with viewer's values]."*

CTA: [Call to Join]
*"Be part of this story. [Action + benefit]."*

### EXAMPLE FULL SCRIPT

**Product**: MealPal Meal Prep Kits

**HOOK (0-5 seconds):**

[Woman in a small apartment kitchen, casual clothes, talking naturally]

"This is Sofia. Three years ago she was a single mom working two jobs and eating gas station food for dinner because she didn't have time to cook."

## ENEMY (5-18 seconds):

"She'd get home at 9pm, kids already in bed, and the fridge was either empty or full of stuff that would take an hour to prep. She tried meal delivery services. $12 a meal, tiny portions, her kids wouldn't eat half of it. She tried meal prepping on Sundays but after working 60 hours a week, spending 4 hours cooking on her one day off felt like punishment. The whole system was built for people with time and money. Not for people like her."

## IGNITION (18-28 seconds):

"One night she was sitting in her car in a Wendy's parking lot, eating a $5 meal before her night shift, and she thought, 'What if the problem isn't that I can't cook. It's that meal prep takes 47 steps when it should take 5.' That night she started designing what became MealPal."

## WINS (28-42 seconds):

"Two years later: 35,000 families use MealPal every week. Average meal prep time went from 4 hours to 25 minutes. 4.8 star rating across 9,000 reviews. Featured in Good Housekeeping, Parents Magazine, and the TODAY Show. Every single recipe is designed to be made by someone who's exhausted, with kids pulling at their leg, using ingredients from any regular grocery store."

## VISION + CTA (42-55 seconds):

"But Sofia's not done. Next year MealPal is partnering with 200 food banks to bring the same system to families who can't afford grocery delivery at all. Her goal is to make home-cooked meals possible for every family, not just the ones with two incomes and a Whole Foods membership. If your evenings feel like a battle between your kids eating well and your own sanity, join the 35,000 families who stopped choosing. Try MealPal free for a week."

**HOOK BANK**

1. "Meet [Name], [unexpected relatable detail that contradicts CEO expectations]"

2. "This is the person behind [Brand], and their story will change how you see [category]"

3. "[Name] lost everything before building [Brand]. Here's what happened."

4. "A [relatable profession] turned a personal frustration into a [X] million dollar company"

5. "The story behind [Brand] starts with [emotional moment]"

6. "Nobody believed in [Name]'s idea. [X] customers later, here's what they built."

7. "This company started in a garage with $200 and a problem nobody was solving"

8. "The founder of [Brand] used to be a [relatable job]. Then everything changed."

9. "Behind every great product is a story. This one starts with [raw moment]."

10. "[Name] didn't start a company. They started a movement. Here's how."
