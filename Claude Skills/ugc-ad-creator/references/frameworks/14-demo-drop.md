# Framework 14: The Demo Drop

Psychology, step breakdown, script template, example script and hook bank.

*Product in Action*

### The Psychology

Seeing is believing. A product demonstration removes the abstract and makes the benefit tangible. The brain processes visual demonstrations 60,000x faster than text claims. When someone SEES the product solving a problem in real time, the trust transfer is immediate.

**Quick Reference: P.R.O.O.F.**

P = Problem in Progress
R = Reveal the Product
O = Operate in Real Time
O = Observe the Result
F = Frame the Features

### DETAILED BREAKDOWN

### P Problem in Progress

Open by showing the problem happening in real time. Don't describe it, SHOW it. The viewer should feel the frustration.

**Example:**

[Character struggling to cut through a tough ingredient with a regular knife, slipping, squishing, making a mess]

### R Reveal the Product

Product enters the frame. No long intro, just bring it in. Make the reveal clean and satisfying.

**Example:**

[Hand picks up the product. Simple. Clean. The viewer knows what's about to happen.]

### O Operate in Real Time

Show the product solving the problem. Let the demonstration speak for itself. Minimal voiceover during the demo, let the visual do the work.

**Example:**

[Product cuts through the ingredient effortlessly. Clean, smooth, satisfying. The contrast with the opening is everything.]

## O Observe the Result

Pause on the result. Let the viewer absorb the difference. This is your "wow" moment.

**Example:**

[Camera holds on the perfect result. Side by side with the mess from before.]

## F Frame the Features

Now explain WHY it works. The demo created curiosity, the feature explanation satisfies it.

**Example:**

"Japanese steel. 67 layers. 15 degree blade angle. That's why it cuts like this."

## SCRIPT TEMPLATE

**HOOK:** [Problem in Progress, show don't tell]

**BODY:**

[Reveal the Product] [Clean entrance into frame]

[Operate in Real Time] [Demonstrate the solution]

[Observe the Result] [Hold on the payoff]

[Frame the Features] "[Feature 1]. [Feature 2]. [Feature 3]. That's why it [performs this way]."

**CTA:**

"[Benefit loaded CTA]."

## EXAMPLE FULL SCRIPT

Product: StainKill Portable Carpet Cleaner

### HOOK (0-5 seconds):

[Close-up of a white couch cushion. A full glass of red wine tips over in slow motion. Wine spreads across the fabric. Camera holds on the stain.]

### REVEAL (5-8 seconds):

[Hand reaches in with StainKill. Small, handheld device. No dramatic intro, just picks it up and positions it over the stain.]

**OPERATE (8-25 seconds):**

[StainKill sprays cleaning solution and immediately suctions. The red wine visibly lifts from the fabric in real time. Camera stays tight on the fabric. No cuts. No speed-up. You watch the stain disappear in one continuous shot. Wine goes from dark red to pink to gone. The suction pulls the liquid into a clear tank on the device so you can SEE the wine leaving the couch.]

**OBSERVE (25-32 seconds):**

[Camera pulls back. The cushion is white again. Person runs their hand over it. Dry. No ring. No shadow. Cut to: the device's dirty water tank, now filled with dark red liquid. All of that was IN the couch.]

**FEATURES (32-42 seconds):**

"Dual-action system. It sprays enzyme cleaner that breaks the stain apart at a molecular level, then instantly suctions everything out. 2,200 PSI suction in something that fits in your hand. The tank is clear so you can see exactly how much it pulled out. Works on wine, coffee, pet accidents, kid disasters, anything liquid that hits fabric. No scrubbing. No blotting. No praying."

**CTA (42-50 seconds):**

"If you own anything white, anything fabric, or anything a kid or pet has access to, you need this in a drawer. Link below."
