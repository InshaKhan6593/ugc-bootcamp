# Framework 16: The Educator

Psychology, step breakdown, script template, example script and hook bank.

*Value First Teaching Ad*

**The Psychology**

When you teach someone something genuinely useful, three things happen: (1) they trust you as an authority, (2) they feel grateful, and (3) they associate the positive learning experience with your brand. This "value first" approach is the highest trust ad format because it gives before it asks.

**Quick Reference: T.E.A.C.H.**

T = Teach Something Real
E = Expand with Depth
A = Align with Your Product
C = Credentialize
H = Hand Off the Action

## DETAILED BREAKDOWN

### T Teach Something Real

Open with a genuinely useful piece of information that your audience didn't know. This should be valuable even if they never buy your product.

**Example:**

*"Your phone screen has 10x more bacteria than a toilet seat. Here's the right way to clean it without damaging the coating."*

*"Most people drink coffee at exactly the wrong time. Your cortisol peaks between 8 and 9am, so caffeine then actually blocks its effect. Try waiting until 10."*

### E Expand with Depth

Go deeper than surface level. The more specific and detailed your teaching, the more credible you become.

**Example:**

*"Here's why: your body's cortisol cycle follows your circadian rhythm. When cortisol is already high, caffeine competes with it instead of complementing it."*

### A Align with Your Product

Naturally connect the teaching to what you sell. The product should feel like the logical tool for applying what they just learned.

**Example:**

"That's why we designed [Coffee Brand] to be maximum effective in that 10 to 11am window. The blend is optimized for [specific mechanism]."

## C Credentialize

Establish why your brand specifically is the authority on this topic. Show your work.

**Example:**

"We worked with 3 chronobiologists to develop this. Published in [Journal]. Tested with [X] users."

## H Hand Off the Action

The CTA should feel like the natural next step in their education, not a hard sell.

**Example:**

"Want to get the most out of your caffeine? Switch your timing first, then try our blend. Link in bio."

## SCRIPT TEMPLATE

**HOOK**: [Teach Something Real]

"[Surprising, genuinely useful fact that provides immediate value]"

**BODY**:

[Expand with Depth] "Here's why: [deeper explanation]. [Additional insight]."

[Align with Your Product] "That's exactly why [Product] was designed to [connection to teaching]."

[Credentialize] "[Authority backing]. [Research]. [Data]."

**CTA**: [Hand Off the Action]

"[Frame CTA as the logical next step in their learning journey]."

## EXAMPLE FULL SCRIPT

**Product**: HydraCell Electrolyte Drink

### HOOK (0-8 seconds):

[Person holding a glass of water, talking to camera like they're sharing a secret]

"You're drinking water wrong. Seriously. If you're drinking 8 glasses of plain water a day and you still feel dehydrated, tired, or get headaches, there's a reason. And it has nothing to do with how MUCH you're drinking."

### DEPTH (8-25 seconds):

"Here's why. Water doesn't hydrate you. Electrolytes do. Your cells absorb water through something called sodium-potassium pumps. Without the right balance of sodium, potassium, and magnesium, water just passes through you. That's why you can drink a gallon of water and still feel thirsty, still get headaches, still feel foggy by 2pm. Your cells are literally letting the water pass through because they don't have the electrolytes to pull it in. It's like trying to fill a bucket with holes in it. The amount doesn't matter if the mechanism is broken."

### ALIGN (25-38 seconds):

[Drops a HydraCell packet into a glass of water]

"That's exactly why HydraCell exists. It's not a sports drink. It's not flavored water. It's the exact ratio of sodium, potassium, and magnesium that your cells need to actually absorb the water you're already drinking. 3x the electrolytes of Gatorade with zero sugar, zero artificial sweeteners, zero dyes. One packet in your morning water and you're getting more real hydration from one glass than most people get from eight."

### CREDENTIALS (38-48 seconds):

"Formulated with two sports medicine doctors and a renal physiologist. The ratio is based on the WHO's oral rehydration science, the same protocol used to save lives in developing countries. Used by 40+ professional sports teams. Tested with over 15,000 users in a 6-month clinical trial."

### CTA (48-58 seconds):

"Step one: stop just drinking more water. Step two: fix the absorption first. One packet of HydraCell in your morning glass. That's it. If you've been tired and dehydrated despite drinking water all day, this is probably why. Link below."
