# Framework 8: The Grit Ad

Psychology, step breakdown, script template, example script and hook bank.

*Lo-Fi / Anti-Production*

### The Psychology

In a sea of polished, high production content, something intentionally rough and raw stands out like a handwritten letter in a pile of junk mail. Meta's own research shows that creator style, lo-fi ads outperform studio produced ads by 63% in direct response campaigns. The imperfection signals authenticity, and authenticity builds trust.

### Quick Reference: 3 RULES

1 = "How can I make this SIMPLER?"
2 = "How can I show this CHEAPLY?"
3 = "What's the OPPOSITE of what competitors do?"

### DETAILED BREAKDOWN

### 1 "How can I make this SIMPLER?"

Strip every sentence down to its skeleton. If you can say it in 5 words, never use 15. Test: Would a 10 year old understand it? Can you delete 70% of the words?

### Example:

*Before: "Our journal helps you process emotions through evidence-based CBT techniques."*
*After: "ANGER JOURNAL + CBT PROMPTS = CALM"*
*Before: "Automatically invest your spare change from everyday purchases"*
*After: "COFFEE CHANGE = STOCKS. SET & FORGET."*

### 2 "How can I show this CHEAPLY?"

Embrace ugly production values. Film with a phone. Use harsh lighting. Write on screen text in a notes app. Use MS Paint style arrows and circles.

### Example:

*Before: Professionally lit chef demonstrating blender.*
*After: Tired parent dumping powder into a water bottle while a toddler yells in the background. Text: "30 SEC = LUNCH DONE."*

## 3 "What's the OPPOSITE of what competitors do?"

Whatever the industry standard ad looks like, do the reverse. If competitors show perfection, show reality. If they use joy, use anger or frustration. If they go long, go brutally short.

**Example:**

*Skincare competitors:* Flawless model applying serum in golden light.
*You:* Extreme close up of real flaky skin + before shot. Text: "THIS IS WHAT ACTUALLY WORKS"
*Fitness competitors:* Shredded athlete with dramatic music.
*You:* Regular person, out of breath, text: "DAY 1 LOOKS LIKE THIS. AND THAT'S FINE."

## SCRIPT TEMPLATE

NOTE: This isn't a step by step framework. It's a set of creative constraints you apply ON TOP of any other framework (SPARK, WOUND, STACK, etc).

STEP 1: Pick any framework from this playbook.
STEP 2: Write your script using that framework.
STEP 3: Apply all 3 rules to strip it down, make it ugly, and flip expectations.

## EXAMPLE FULL SCRIPT

**Product:** FitPlan Home Workout App (using GRIT rules on top of PRESSURE COOKER framework)

**VISUAL STYLE:**

Shot on phone. Kitchen floor. No ring light. Dog walks through frame. Text overlays in iPhone Notes screenshot style.

**HOOK (0-3 seconds):**

[Person on kitchen floor in old sweats, out of breath, sweaty]

Text on screen: "DAY 1. THIS IS WHAT $0/MONTH LOOKS LIKE."

**BODY (3-18 seconds):**

[Still on floor, talking to phone propped against a cabinet]

"I cancelled my gym membership 4 months ago because who has time to drive 20 minutes, work out for an hour, drive back, and shower? That's 2 hours for 45 minutes of exercise. So I downloaded this app and I've been doing 20-minute workouts on my kitchen floor. No equipment. No shoes. My toddler climbs on me during planks. It's chaos."

[Cut to phone screen showing the app, filmed shaky, casual]

"But I'm down 11 pounds in 4 months. And I haven't missed a single day because 'missing a day' would mean not using my own floor for 20 minutes."

### CTA (18-25 seconds):

Text on screen: "GYM = $50/mo + 2 hrs. THIS = $0 + 20 min. DO THE MATH."

[Person waves at camera, still on the floor]

"Link's in the bio or whatever. I'm gonna go lie here for a minute."

## HOOK BANK

1. "Do you [emotional trigger]? Read this." (text on crumpled paper)

2. "[Screenshot of a notes app with one punchy line]"

3. "My therapist said I should write this down. So here:" (handwritten on paper)

4. "[Problem] in 2024 vs 2025" (crude side by side comparison)

5. "I made this in 5 minutes because it needed to be said"

6. "[Screenshot of text conversation about the product]"

7. "No fancy edit. No script. Just the truth about [product]."

8. "I filmed this on my phone in my kitchen because you need to see this"

9. "This isn't an ad. This is me telling you what actually works."

10. "[Handwritten note] Dear [audience], stop buying [competitor type]. Sincerely, someone who wasted $[X]."
