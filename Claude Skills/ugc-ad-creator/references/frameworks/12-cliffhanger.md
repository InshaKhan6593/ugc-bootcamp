# Framework 12: The Cliffhanger

Psychology, step breakdown, script template, example script and hook bank.

*Curiosity Loop*

## The Psychology

A curiosity loop opens a mental "gap" in the viewer's mind that demands resolution. The brain literally can't rest until the loop is closed. This is the same mechanism that makes you finish a bad movie just to see the ending. In advertising, it's one of the most powerful retention tools available.

**Quick Reference: L.O.O.P.**

L = Launch the Impossible Claim
O = Orchestrate the Story
O = Offer the Value
P = Pay Off the Promise

## DETAILED BREAKDOWN

### L Launch the Impossible Claim

Open with a statement that seems too good, too weird, or too contradictory to be true. The viewer MUST keep watching to make sense of it.

**Example:**

*"I got my rent paid this month by buying a $12 candle"*
*"My boss gave me a promotion because I stopped working overtime"*

### O Orchestrate the Story

Build a narrative that slowly moves toward resolving the opening claim. Each beat should add context without yet explaining the hook.

**Example:**

*"So here's what happened. I saw this candle brand on Instagram and thought it looked nice. But when I went to the website, I noticed their affiliate program..."*

### O Offer the Value

Within the story, naturally demonstrate the product's benefits. Let the advantages emerge from the narrative rather than being listed.

**Example:**

"The candle itself smelled incredible, my whole apartment was different. But the real thing was the affiliate link. I shared it with my followers and by the end of the month..."

## P Pay Off the Promise

Close the loop. Reveal the resolution that makes the opening claim make sense. The "aha" moment should feel clever and satisfying.

**Example:**

"...I'd earned $1,400 in commissions. More than my rent. From a $12 candle purchase."

## SCRIPT TEMPLATE

HOOK: [Launch the Impossible Claim]

"[Surprising/contradictory statement that demands explanation]"

BODY:

[Orchestrate the Story] "Here's what happened: [Story beat 1]. [Story beat 2]. [Story beat 3]."
[Offer the Value] "[Natural product experience woven into story]. [Benefit discovered through narrative]."
[Pay Off the Promise] "[Resolution that makes the opening claim logical and satisfying]."

CTA:

"[CTA that makes the viewer feel they can replicate the story]."

## EXAMPLE FULL SCRIPT

Product: SkillStack Online Course Platform

### HOOK (0-5 seconds):

[Person sitting at a nice desk setup, looking at camera with a slight smile]

"I quit my $95,000 a year job because of a $29 online course. And my income went up."

### STORY (5-25 seconds):

"So here's what happened. I was a marketing manager at a tech company. Fine job, fine pay, fine everything. But I'd been freelancing on the side doing video editing for small businesses. Nothing crazy, just weekend projects. Then a friend told me about SkillStack. I took their 'Freelance Video Business' course thinking I'd pick up a few tips."

The course wasn't just editing techniques. It was how to price your work, how to build a client pipeline, how to package services so clients come to you instead of the other way around. I rewrote all my proposals using their framework. I raised my prices by 60%."

**VALUE (25-40 seconds):**

"Within 3 months my freelance income matched my salary. But here's the part that surprised me. The course had a module on building a team and delegating. I hired two editors, trained them using the exact workflows from the course, and started taking on bigger clients. Suddenly I wasn't just an editor. I was running a production company from my apartment."

**PAYOFF (40-50 seconds):**

"I quit my full-time job in March. It's September now and I've made $148,000 this year. From a $29 course. Not because the course is magic, but because it gave me the business framework I was missing. I knew how to edit. I didn't know how to run a business. That $29 filled a gap worth $50,000."

**CTA (50-60 seconds):**

"If you have a skill but you're stuck trading hours for dollars, the business framework is what you're missing. SkillStack courses start at $29. Best investment I've ever made and I'm not even exaggerating. Link below."
