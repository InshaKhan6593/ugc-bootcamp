# Viral Short-Form

You can have the best characters, the best visuals and the best ads, and none of it matters if nobody sees them. Reach is not luck - it is a set of signals the platform reads, and almost all of them trace back to one thing: **does the viewer keep watching, and do they react?**

Everything in this skill is downstream of that. Hooks exist to buy the next second. Retention structure exists to keep buying seconds. Engagement tactics exist because comments tell the algorithm the video is alive. Posting rules exist so the signals are readable instead of noisy.

## Critical rules

1. **Watch time is the metric that decides everything else.** If people leave at two seconds, nothing about hashtags, captions or posting time will save the video. Fix the opening first.
2. **The first clip should not exceed about three seconds.** If the viewer has to wait five seconds for something to change, they are gone.
3. **One niche, no exceptions.** Not even one off-topic video. The algorithm builds an audience model from your content and your own viewing; mixed signals mean it stops knowing who to show you to.
4. **Post consistently at a fixed time**, 3-5 times a day maximum, and never twice within an hour. Fixed timing is what makes your metrics comparable week to week.
5. **Ride momentum.** When a video performs, post again soon rather than waiting - viewers on the hit will find the newer post, and the newer post inherits the attention.
6. **Copy what is working, do not reinvent it.** If a video in your niche went up yesterday and has millions of views, make your version with a few changes and post it now. Speed matters more than originality here.
7. **Make the page look like a page.** Consistent thumbnails, a clear bio with a reason to follow, and a coherent grid all convert a viewer into a follower.
8. **Post everywhere.** The hard part was making the video. Uploading the same asset to TikTok, Reels and Shorts takes minutes and multiplies reach.
9. **Never delete videos - make them private instead.** Platforms treat deletion poorly; archiving achieves the same thing without the penalty.
10. **Upload at the platform's preferred spec, not the highest numbers.** Exporting 4K60 gets re-encoded and looks worse than a clean 1080p30 upload.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Platform and positioning
Pick a primary platform and a niche narrow enough to be recognisable. Geography is a lever too: content made for a specific market, with the matching references and hashtags, gets pushed to viewers there - which matters if you are optimising earnings per thousand views. Details in `references/platform-and-posting.md`.

### Step 2: Channel branding
Decide the bio (clear, specific, with a reason to follow and a follower goal if it suits), the thumbnail treatment that will repeat across every post, the handle and the grid logic. This is a one-time decision that compounds, and it is what turns views into follows.

### Step 3: Engineer the hook and the retention curve
Write the hook before the video. Then structure for the drop-off points rather than for a beginning, middle and end: something changes in the first three seconds, a reason to stay at five, a reason at ten, a payoff before the end, and a reason to rewatch. Hook patterns, retention structures and the text-on-screen rule are in `references/hooks-and-retention.md`.

### Step 4: Produce
Build the content with the craft skills - `ai-image-prompting`, `ai-cinematography`, `ai-video-prompting` - and for a proven format, follow the niche-cloning workflow in `references/viral-niches.md`, which includes a prompt pack covering POV, animal fusion, miniature, cats, knights, sea monsters, fruit creations and anthropomorphic-character niches.

### Step 5: Post properly
Fixed time, correct export spec, up to four hashtags, text on screen, captions where they help, screenshot to your story. Full upload checklist in `references/platform-and-posting.md`. Tick the AI-content disclosure - many large accounts do not and still get reach, but the labelling cost is trivial next to the account risk.

### Step 6: Work the first hour
The window right after posting is where early engagement decides distribution. Reply to every comment with a question or a twist rather than a thank-you, turning one comment into three. Build a small group of creators in your niche who comment on each other's posts. Engage with niche content daily so the platform knows which audience you belong to. Tactics, including the ones worth avoiding, are in `references/engagement-tactics.md`.

### Step 7: Read the metrics and iterate
Watch where people drop off, not just the view count. A two-second cliff is a hook problem; a five-second slide is a pacing problem; good retention with no follows is a page problem. Change one variable per post so the data means something. The diagnostic table is in `references/account-health.md`.

## Output format
Match the ask:

- **Hooks:** ten options, varied in type, with the strongest marked and why.
- **A video plan:** hook, beat-by-beat retention structure with timings, on-screen text, sound, and the production notes.
- **A posting plan:** cadence, times, hashtags, caption, cross-posting list.
- **A diagnosis:** what the metric says, the single most likely cause, and the one change to make next post.
- **A niche clone:** the format broken into its repeatable parts, then prompts for the first batch.

Keep it actionable. One video they can make today beats a strategy document.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Most likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Views die at 2 seconds | The hook, or a slow first clip | Open mid-action; first clip under 3 seconds; put the payoff promise first |
| Steady slide through the middle | Pacing and no reason to stay | Add a change every few seconds - cut, reveal, text, escalation |
| Good retention, no followers | The page, not the video | Clear bio, consistent thumbnails, a reason to follow |
| Good views, no comments | Nothing to react to | Add an opinion, a question, or one deliberately odd detail people point out |
| Views dropped across the account | Off-niche posts, or posting too often | Return to one niche; space posts 1-2 hours apart; 3-5 a day maximum |
| New account gets zero views | Not warmed up | Watch niche content for about a week, post a throwaway video, check whether follows register - see `references/account-health.md` |
| Quality looks worse after upload | Wrong export spec | 1080p30 rather than 4K60 |
| Reach fell after a trip abroad | Location signal changed | Avoid logging in from another country on the account's device; see `references/account-health.md` |
| Audience is the wrong country | Language and content signals | Make content for the target market, use its hashtags, do not reply in other languages |
| Nothing works despite good content | No consistency or no momentum | Fixed posting time, daily cadence, and post again immediately when something lands |
