# Cloning a viral niche

The real skill is not knowing today's trending formats - it is being able to take any format you see working and reproduce it within a day. Formats rotate constantly; the deconstruction method does not.

## How to deconstruct any viral format

Watch three or four videos from the account and answer six questions:

1. **What is the repeatable formula?** Every viral niche is one sentence with a variable in it: *tiny workers inspecting a giant [food]*, *an anthropomorphic [animal] in a suit at night*, *a knight who personifies [emotion]*, *first-person POV of [historical moment]*.
2. **What stays identical across posts?** The style, the lighting, the framing, the scale relationship. This is what makes the account recognisable.
3. **What varies?** Usually exactly one thing - the food, the animal, the emotion, the era. One variable is what makes it a format rather than a series of ideas.
4. **What is the hook frame?** The image that stops the scroll, which is almost always the one that shows the strange relationship immediately.
5. **What is the motion?** Usually minimal - a slow push, a slow pan, a small action. These formats live on stills brought barely to life.
6. **What is the sound?** Trending audio, a narrated line, or ambient. Often the same sound every time.

Write those six answers down and you have a production template.

## The clone workflow

1. **Find a format working right now in a niche you can tolerate making for months.** If a video in your niche went up yesterday with millions of views, make your version today. Do not try to reinvent it - make it, post it, and change one thing.
2. **Write the formula sentence** with the variable marked.
3. **Build the first prompt** at full quality, then test two variables on it.
4. **Generate a batch of 10-20 stills** by swapping only the variable.
5. **Gate them** - keep the ones where the strange relationship reads instantly at thumbnail size.
6. **Animate minimally** - a slow push in, a slow orbit, or one small action. Over-animating these formats breaks them.
7. **Assemble** with consistent sound, consistent text treatment, consistent pacing.
8. **Post daily on the format** until the numbers tell you which variable the audience prefers, then make more of that.

The production craft - prompt structure, lighting, camera, motion - lives in `ai-image-prompting`, `ai-cinematography` and `ai-video-prompting`. This file is only about the format layer.

## Why these formats work

Understanding the mechanism lets you invent your own instead of waiting for the next one:

| Mechanism | What it does | Formats built on it |
|---|---|---|
| Scale violation | The brain cannot resolve the size relationship, so it keeps looking | Miniature workers, giant creatures, sea monsters |
| Category violation | Two things that cannot coexist, coexisting | Animal hybrids, animal fusion, anthropomorphic characters in suits |
| Embodiment | The viewer is placed inside the moment | POV niches |
| Personification | An abstract idea given a body | Knights as emotions, concepts as characters |
| Transformation | Something becomes something else in sequence | Evolutions, before-and-after |
| Familiar made strange | An ordinary object treated with total seriousness | Fruit creations, food as architecture |
| Access | A place or moment nobody gets to see | Stadium cam, live film set |

A new niche is usually one of these mechanisms applied to a subject that has not had it applied yet.

## Prompt pack

Adapt these; do not post them as-is. Each one shows the formula with its variable in place.

### Miniature niche (scale violation)
> An HD image, with very good lighting, there are tiny workers inspecting a massive **[banana / stack of pancakes / cucumber slices / sandwich / bunch of grapes]**, the size of a house. You can see all the workers inspecting the food, from different places. The workers are miniature next to the food. They have scaffolding and trucks.

Keep: the scale relationship, the industrial props, the clean bright light. Vary: the food only. Note how plain the prompt is - the idea carries it, not the adjectives.

### Anthropomorphic character niche (category violation)
> A hyper-realistic anthropomorphic **[rat / black panther / pig / white panther]** wearing **[a black suit, white shirt and black tie]**, standing on a rooftop at night with a glowing full moon behind him, wearing sleek black sunglasses, smoking a cigarette with visible smoke trails, intense mafia gangster vibe, dramatic lighting, cinematic city skyline in the background, noir atmosphere, dark colour palette, ultra-realism, stylish and moody.

Keep: the wardrobe formula, the night setting, the noir lighting. Vary: the animal, the city, the time of night.

### POV niche (embodiment)
> First-person POV, **[inside a rattling steel landing craft, boots soaked, rifle shaking in your hands, the ramp drops]** - blinding flashes, deafening gunfire, soldiers rushing into rough crashing waves, bullets slicing through the air, beach obstacles silhouetted against grey smoke, shouting officers barely audible, chaotic cinematography, cinematic war lens, ultra-realism, fog of war atmosphere.

> POV shot, first-person perspective, **1980s basement**, dim warm lighting, hands gripping a classic game controller, CRT TV displaying a pixelated retro game, glass bottles on shag carpet, a greasy pizza box with slices missing, wood-panelled walls, VHS tapes and board games stacked nearby, friends lounging in bean bag chairs (ultra realistic facial features), vintage speakers and posters in the background, soft cinematic glow, hyper-realistic, nostalgic 35mm film grain.

Keep: the first-person framing with the viewer's hands or legs visible in the foreground, and the sensory detail list. Vary: the moment. The hands in frame are what make POV read as POV.

### Knights as emotions (personification)
> A hyper-realistic dark fantasy knight known as **"[Anger]"**, clad in **[scorched steel plate armour glowing with cracks of molten orange]**, full-face helmet shaped like **[a clenched jaw]**, **[plume of ash and flame trailing from his shoulders]**, **[two-handed greatsword igniting with fire]** in his grip, armour scratched and dented from constant battle, standing in **[a battlefield of blackened earth and smouldering wreckage]**, smoke and sparks filling the air, cinematic volcanic lighting, brutal medieval realism, 4K detail.

Keep: the five-slot structure - name, armour material and colour, helmet shape, weapon, environment - plus the realism and lighting tail. Vary: the emotion, and let every slot follow from it. Fear gets a featureless mask and fog; Disgust gets moss, rust and swamp.

### Sea monsters (scale violation)
> A hyper-realistic scene of a colossal **[beached octopus with multiple eyes]**, sprawled across the deck of an industrial fishing vessel, surrounded by stunned construction workers in yellow hard hats and orange jumpsuits, overcast sky above a cold grey sea, cranes and ropes hooked into the creature's limbs, gritty wet steel textures, maritime atmosphere, the scale of the creature dwarfing the humans, cinematic sci-fi realism, deep-sea horror meets industrial realism.

Keep: the human reference objects that prove scale - workers, hard hats, cranes - and the documentary grading. Vary: the creature.

### Animal fusion and hybrids (category violation)
Formula: one recognisable animal's body with another's defining feature, photographed as if it were ordinary wildlife documentation. The realism of the treatment is what sells the impossibility, so keep the lighting and camera language plain and naturalistic.

### Fruit creations, cats, Tim Cheese, stadium cam, live film set
Same method: write the one-sentence formula, keep the treatment identical, vary one thing. The full original prompt pack with multiple worked examples per niche is in the course material at `Phase 7 - Social Media & Going Viral/07 - What's This For/Prompts PDF.md`.

## Adapting a prompt to a new subject

The fastest route is to hand the chat model an existing prompt and ask for a transposition:

> Copy this style of prompt exactly, but change **[the animal / the food / the emotion / the era]** to **[X]**. Keep the lighting, framing, realism cues and structure identical.

Then ask for a batch:

> Give me 15 versions of this prompt, varying only **[the variable]**. Keep every other word the same.

That is the whole production line for a niche account: one strong prompt, one variable, fifteen outputs, posted daily.

## Turning stills into posts

- **One still per post** works for image-led niches, with a slow push-in or a parallax move to make it a video.
- **A set of three to five stills** on the same formula makes a stronger post: the same idea applied to different variables, cut fast, reads as a format rather than a one-off.
- **Minimal motion.** A slow push, a slow orbit, or one small action. These formats break when over-animated.
- **Same sound every time.** Format accounts benefit from an audio signature as much as a visual one.
- **Text on screen naming the variable** - "Anger", "the grape", "1944" - gives the viewer the formula instantly and makes the series legible.
- **Consistent thumbnails.** For a format account this is most of the follow decision; see `platform-and-posting.md`.

One caution on realism: AI content in these niches draws "this is AI" comments, which break the illusion for everyone reading. The keyword-filter approach in `engagement-tactics.md` is the standard handling, and it keeps the engagement while hiding the pile-on.
