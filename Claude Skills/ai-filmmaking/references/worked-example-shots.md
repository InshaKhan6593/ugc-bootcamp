## Example A: 14s cold open, selfie camera, three characters

**References, in order:** @1 character D-LOW, @2 his BMX, @3 character GOLDIE, @4 her car, @5 character KENJI, @6 his motorcycle, @7 boombox, @8 location plate.

> 35mm Kodak 500T. handheld dynamic cinematography. Low golden-hour sun, warm skin tones, cool skylight on the shadow side. Photorealistic live-action, raw documentary realism. No 3D render, no game engine, no plastic skin, no retouch, no oversharpening, no HDR grading. 24fps, 180-degree shutter blur. SFX only no music. No subtitles. American accent. 14s.
>
> **Scene context:** A quiet LA cul-de-sac at golden hour, a crew of creators on their block. The man with the headband films himself on his own small camera, showing off to the lens. The blonde woman across the street undercuts him without raising her voice. Nobody travels anywhere.
>
> @image1 - Black man, black paisley headband, full black beard, olive bomber with orange BC monogram, white tee, silver chain, baggy dark jeans. Neck and chest tattoos read as dark texture only, never a legible drawing. @image2 - his BMX: matte dark-green scuffed frame, black parts, 4 pegs. @image3 - blonde woman, dark roots, high long ponytail, narrow silver-framed smoke-tinted sunglasses, hoops, olive knit crop top with orange BC monogram, denim shorts. @image4 - the car: graphite low coupe, long hood, fastback roof, chrome multi-spoke wheels. @image5 - East Asian man, black hair in a low ponytail, colour irezumi neck collar, small fox tattoo on right cheekbone, same olive bomber. @image6 - motorcycle: glossy black supersport, orange pinstripes and rim tape. @image7 - silver 1980s cassette boombox, round speakers, orange tuner stripe. @image8 - location only: LA cul-de-sac, cream and sand stucco houses, tall bare-trunk palms, cracked asphalt, poles and wires, dry lawns. All cards give appearance ONLY. Do NOT reproduce sheet layout, multiple views, panels, captions, grey backdrop or catalogue pose.
>
> **STRICT: EXACTLY THREE PEOPLE.** @image1 = ONLY black headband and beard. @image3 = ONLY blonde ponytail and tinted glasses. @image5 = ONLY ponytail and tattooed neck. Never duplicate, never add a fourth person.
>
> **CAMERA:** ONE CONTINUOUS TAKE, no cuts, no change of framing. The camera is the small one in @image1's own outstretched right hand, selfie framing, 22mm, gentle barrel distortion at the edges. He is STANDING STILL and so is the camera position - the only motion is the live weight of his arm: vertical bobbing with his breathing, small lateral sway, occasional roll tilts, fine micro-jitter, and instinctive corrections keeping HIS FACE IN FRAME and readable. He sits in the RIGHT THIRD, the left two thirds open on the depth of the street. The background is fixed and does not travel: houses, palms and parked cars hold the same position in frame throughout.
>
> **STAGING:** @image1 stands planted on the asphalt, weight rocking foot to foot. RIGHT hand holds the camera at arm's length. LEFT hand is free, gesturing loosely as he talks, then dropping. @image2 lies FLAT ON ITS SIDE on the asphalt at his feet, front wheel turned, one pedal on the ground, shadow tight under it. Mid-ground left: @image4 PARKED at the kerb, engine off, completely stationary, wheels not turning. @image3 sits on its roof, one knee up, other leg hanging down the door; in her LEFT hand a light wooden baseball bat stands VERTICALLY, knob on the roof beside her hip, palm capping the end like a cane. @image7 sits on the ground by the front wheel, playing. @image5 leans on the front wing of that car, arms folded, weight on one hip, calm and silent. @image6 stands further back on its side stand. All three are continuously, idly alive - weight shifting, fabric and hair in the breeze - never statue-still.
>
> **ACTION, TIMED:**
> 0.0-1.2s Frame is BLACK because his palm is pressed flat over the lens. The palm slides away and the street floods in. Not a fade.
> 1.2-2.2s Framing settles on his face. He is already mid-thought, into the lens.
> 2.2-4.0s @image1, grinning down the lens: "Some people make this look difficult."
> 4.0-4.8s In depth @image3 turns her head and finds the camera, looking past him into the lens, not at him.
> 4.8-7.6s @image3, flat and dry, chewing between words: "He rehearsed that line in the mirror. Changed shirts twice."
> 7.6-8.4s @image1 turns his HEAD sharply left toward her, then straight back to the lens.
> 8.4-9.6s @image1, unbothered: "That's called preparation."
> 9.6-11.4s @image1, to the lens: "She gets mean when she's impressed."
> 11.4-14.0s He holds the lens, weight shifting, left hand settling. @image3 holds the lens too, jaw working the gum.
> Do not rush the dialogue. Leave silence between lines.
>
> **ACTING:** @image1's eyes are fully visible, uneven natural blinking, broad easy grin AU6+AU12. At 4.8s, on her words, the grin CRACKS for half a second - AU4 brows pull in, one lip corner tightens AU14 - then reasserts wider than before, a faint AU4 trace behind it. A man taking a hit and covering it, never a comic mug. @image3's eyes sit behind tinted lenses, so it all plays in her brows, nostrils, jaw, mouth and small tilts of the chin. Fully deadpan, never smiles, never seeks a reaction. Identity and skin texture stay constant.
>
> ONLY THE SCRIPTED LINES ARE SPOKEN. No ad-libs, no mumbling, no grunts, no unscripted sighs or breaths-as-voice, no chuckles, no overlapping speech, no offscreen voices. Mouths closed before and after their lines. @image5 never speaks.
>
> **SFX** - wind past the mic, distant traffic, dry palm fronds, shoe scuff on grit, the boombox playing thin and small across the street.
>
> **STRICT NEGATIVES:** no tripod, gimbal, dolly or crane, no stabilised or smooth camera. No slow motion, no speed ramp. No fade from black, the palm reveals the frame. NOTHING IN THE SHOT DRIVES OR ROLLS: the parked car never moves, drifts or follows him, its wheels never turn; the motorcycle never moves. @image1 never rides, never mounts the BMX, never picks it up, never walks out of position, never lowers the camera. @image3 never stands, never leaves the roof, never puts the bat down, no bubble here. @image5 never unfolds his arms, never walks. No relighting, no change of time of day or weather.

### What is doing the work

| Line | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| "The camera is the small one in @image1's own outstretched right hand" | Makes the camera a diegetic object with a physical owner, which is why the handheld motion is specific instead of generic shake |
| "He is STANDING STILL and so is the camera position" | Separates arm movement from camera travel - the distinction the model would otherwise collapse |
| "The background is fixed and does not travel" | Kills the default slow drift without using the word "static" |
| "Neck tattoos read as dark texture only, never a legible drawing" | Prevents invented artwork that changes every shot |
| "no bubble here" | A negative aimed at a specific beat that happens in a *different* shot - cross-shot discipline |
| "Not a fade" | The model's instinct for a black opening is a fade; this forces the physical reveal |

## Example B: 20s one-take, camera walking backwards, one character

**References:** @1 character PAPA DOC, @2 his brick phone, @3 his house (start of shot only), @4 the street he walks into.

> 35mm Kodak 500T. handheld dynamic cinematography. Low golden-hour sun, warm skin tones, cool skylight on shadow side. Photorealistic live-action, raw documentary realism. No 3D render, no game engine, no plastic skin. 24fps, 180-degree shutter blur. SFX only no music. No subtitles. American accent. 20s.
>
> **Scene context:** A quiet LA cul-de-sac at golden hour. A big man comes out of his front door mid-phone-call, down the steps, past the car on his driveway and out onto the street, still talking. Annoyed, businesslike, unhurried. The camera walks backwards in front of him and the street opens up behind his shoulders. One unbroken take - the comedy lives in never cutting it.
>
> @image1 - very large heavyset Black man, black bowler hat, thick black-rimmed glasses, clear lenses, short greying beard, mole on left cheek, olive short-sleeve shirt with orange BC on the pocket, baggy jeans. @image2 - phone: black 1980s brick handset, long rubber antenna, grey keypad, scuffed. @image3 - HIS HOUSE, start of shot only: peach stucco bungalow, brown shingled roof, white metal security screen door with scrollwork, low concrete steps, driveway with a silver sedan parked on it, green shrubs, tall palm trunk right. @image4 - THE STREET he walks into: same cul-de-sac, cream stucco houses, tall bare-trunk palms, cracked asphalt, poles, dry lawns. All cards give appearance ONLY. Do NOT reproduce sheet layout, multiple views, panels, captions or grey backdrop.
>
> **STRICT: EXACTLY ONE PERSON IN THE WHOLE SHOT.** @image1 = ONLY bowler hat and heavy build. The street behind him is completely empty of people: no crew, no neighbours, no pedestrians, no extras, no duplicates.
>
> **CAMERA** - ONE CONTINUOUS TAKE, NO CUTS, NO SECOND ANGLE. 35mm, chest height, shoulder-mounted handheld walking BACKWARDS in front of him for all 20 seconds, retreating at exactly his pace so he stays the same size in frame. Real operator weight: a soft vertical step-bounce per stride, small roll, tiny reframing corrections, never smooth or mechanical. He fills the frame chest-up and always faces the lens. As the camera retreats his house falls away behind him and THE STREET opens up BEHIND HIS SHOULDERS - first driveway and palm, then house fronts, then the far end of the empty cul-de-sac. Parallax proves the move: the near palm sweeps past, poles cross slower, far houses barely drift.
>
> **STAGING:** @image1 holds @image2 to his right ear the entire take, left hand loose, gesturing once or twice. The silver sedan sits PARKED on his driveway, engine off, wheels never turning; he walks around it. Ordinary neighbourhood cars are parked along the kerbs in depth - scenery only, nobody in them, none of them ever move.
>
> **ACTION, TIMED:**
> 0.0-2.0s The white security screen door swings open and @image1 comes out, phone already at his ear, mid-conversation, and starts down the concrete steps. He is walking before we ever see him still.
> 2.0-3.2s @image1, flat and final: "Sunset. Under the bridge."
> 3.2-4.7s He listens, moving down the driveway past the sedan, straight at the lens. Jaw setting.
> 4.7-10.1s @image1, hard and irritated, building: "Tell that smug punk Johnny to get himself down there - and bring the prototype he stole like the little rat he is."
> 10.1-11.3s He listens, brows drawing together. He shakes his head once, slowly, and steps off the driveway onto the street.
> 11.3-13.7s @image1, tired and pointed: "And don't let that clown Mumbles write the address down."
> 13.7-14.7s He listens, then his eyes close for a beat and open again - remembering something he wishes he could forget.
> 14.7-17.3s @image1, dry and weary, almost to himself: "Last time I gave him four digits, he came back with six."
> 17.3-20.0s He hangs up with his thumb, drops @image2 to his hip and keeps walking at the lens, the whole street now open behind him.
>
> **ACTING:** @image1's eyes are fully visible behind clear lenses; he never performs to camera. AU4 furrow deepening through the second line, nostrils flaring once on "stole", jaw working, AU17 chin raise on the Mumbles line, then flat weariness on the last.
>
> ONLY @image1 SPEAKS AND ONLY THE LINES ABOVE. The person on the other end is NEVER heard - no voice from the phone, no audio leak, no reply. No ad-libs, mumbling, grunts, unscripted sighs or breaths-as-voice, chuckles or offscreen voices. Each line lands on the tail of the beat before it; no dead air, no waiting, speech never sped up or compressed.
>
> **SFX** - the screen door swinging shut behind him, footsteps on concrete then asphalt, wind past the mic, dry palm fronds, a distant BMX freewheel, far traffic.
>
> **STRICT NEGATIVES:** no cut, insert, reverse angle, close-up of the phone, shot of whoever he is talking to, flashback, or imagery of Johnny or Mumbles - they are audio only and never appear. The camera never stops, overtakes him, circles him or gets behind his shoulder. No tripod, gimbal, dolly, crane or drone. No zoom, push-in or rack focus. No slow motion or speed ramp. The silver sedan never moves, wheels never turn; no parked car in the street ever moves. No relighting or change of time of day.

### What is doing the work

| Line | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| "retreating at exactly his pace so he stays the same size in frame" | Defines the move as a relationship rather than a speed, which is how a backwards walk actually works |
| "Parallax proves the move: the near palm sweeps past, poles cross slower, far houses barely drift" | Tells the model what the move should *look* like, not just what it is - this is the line that stops a fake slide |
| "He is walking before we ever see him still" | Starts the shot mid-action, which removes the dead opening frame models love to add |
| "@image3 - HIS HOUSE, start of shot only" | Scopes a reference in time, so the house does not persist into the second half |
| "no flashback, or imagery of Johnny or Mumbles - they are audio only and never appear" | Named characters in dialogue are an invitation for the model to visualise them |
| Dialogue beats of 1.2 to 5.4 seconds | Lines sized to be speakable; the long one is the angry one, where speed is motivated |

## What both have in common

1. **The look block is identical, word for word.** Two very different shots, one unedited paragraph of visual DNA.
2. **One continuous take.** Twenty seconds in one generation instead of five cuts and five continuity risks.
3. **A reason stated for the camera's behaviour** - it is in his hand, it is on a shoulder walking backwards. Models comply better with a physical cause than with an adjective.
4. **Timed beats that start mid-action and never leave dead air.**
5. **Acting written as physical movement plus subtext**, never as an emotion word on its own.
6. **A speech-isolation block** naming exactly who talks and forbidding everyone else's lips from moving.
7. **Negatives aimed at this shot's specific temptations**, not a generic quality list.
8. **Vehicles explicitly parked and inert.** In both shots. Every single time.
