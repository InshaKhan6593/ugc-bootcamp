Welcome to Phase 6. This is where you’ll learn how to use Flows, our node-based system for building AI creation workflows.
Don’t worry, this might look a little different at first, but you do not need to understand everything straight away. This phase is here to show you how to connect simple steps together, one piece at a time.
You’ll learn how nodes work & why we believe this is the best/fastest way to make content & organise your creative thoughts in 2026.
Think of Flows as your creative workspace.
Instead of jumping between tools and losing track of what you’re doing, you are able to build a clear process all in one place.
