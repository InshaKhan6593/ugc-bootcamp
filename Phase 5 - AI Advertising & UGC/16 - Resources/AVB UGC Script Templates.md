## What Makes Great UGC

*It has to feel like a real person, not a brand*

UGC (User Generated Content) works because it doesn't look like an ad. It looks like a real person talking to their friend about something they love and AI can now recreate it perfectly. The scripts below are designed to sound natural, not scripted. Replace anything in [BRACKETS] with your product details.

<!-- page 3 -->

## Script 1: The Unboxing

*Best for: Physical products, subscription boxes, tech gadgets*

HOOK: "Okay this just arrived and I'm literally so excited"

BODY:

"So I've been waiting for [PRODUCT NAME] to come in and... look at this packaging. [React to packaging]

Let me show you what's inside. [Open and reveal]

Okay so this is [describe what you see and first impressions]. The thing that got me to buy this was [KEY BENEFIT]. And honestly? [Give genuine reaction].

[Show product in use or up close]

The quality is [honest assessment]. For [PRICE POINT] this is actually [value judgment]."

CTA: "Link is in [their] bio if you want to check it out. Trust me on this one."

## Script 2: The Testimonial

*Best for: Services, courses, software, results-based products*

HOOK: "I never do these but I genuinely have to talk about [PRODUCT/SERVICE]"

BODY:

"So here's the deal. [TIMEFRAME] ago I was [BEFORE STATE]. I'm talking [specific detail about the struggle].

Then I found [PRODUCT/SERVICE] and honestly I was skeptical at first. [Why you were skeptical].

But [TIMEFRAME] later? [AFTER STATE]. Like I went from [specific before metric] to [specific after metric].

The thing that makes it different is [UNIQUE SELLING POINT]. I've tried [COMPETITOR/ALTERNATIVE] before and it wasn't even close."

CTA: "If you're dealing with [PROBLEM], just try it. Worst case you [LOW RISK FACTOR]. Best case it changes everything."

## Script 3: Day In My Life

*Best for: Lifestyle products, apps, supplements, daily-use items*

HOOK: "Day in my life with [PRODUCT] (this changed my routine)"

BODY:

"[Morning/start of day shot] So the first thing I do is [MORNING ROUTINE WITH PRODUCT].

[Mid-day shot] Then throughout the day I [HOW PRODUCT FITS INTO DAILY LIFE].

The reason I started using this is [ORIGINAL PROBLEM]. And now it's just... part of my day, you know?

[Show specific moment of use] This is my favorite part because [SPECIFIC BENEFIT].

[End of day] Honestly at this point I can't imagine [ROUTINE] without it."

CTA: "If you want to try it, [WHERE TO GET IT]. You won't regret it."

## Script 4: Problem-Solution

*Best for: Pain-point driven products, anything that solves a clear issue*

HOOK: "If you deal with [PROBLEM], stop scrolling"

BODY:

"I used to [DESCRIBE THE STRUGGLE]. Like every single [TIME PERIOD] I'd [SPECIFIC ANNOYING THING].

I tried [THING THAT DIDN'T WORK]. I tried [ANOTHER THING]. Nothing actually fixed it.

Then someone told me about [PRODUCT] and I was like... really? [Express initial doubt]

[Show product] But look. [DEMONSTRATE THE SOLUTION]. That's it. That's literally all you have to do.

It's been [TIMEFRAME] and I haven't had [PROBLEM] since."

CTA: "Link in bio. You're welcome."

## Script 5: Before/After

<!-- page 5 -->

*Best for: Beauty, fitness, cleaning products, design tools, any visual transformation*

HOOK: "Watch this transformation" or "Before vs after [PRODUCT]"

BODY:

"[Show BEFORE state] Okay so this is what [THING] looked like before. [Describe the problem visually]

[Show applying/using product] All I did was [SIMPLE STEPS]. Literally took me [SHORT TIME].

[Show AFTER state] And now look at this. [React to the result]

I'm not even exaggerating, this is [HONEST REACTION]. The difference is [SPECIFIC COMPARISON]."

CTA: "[PRODUCT NAME], go get it. Link in bio."

## Script 6: Get Ready With Me (GRWM)

*Best for: Beauty, fashion, accessories, lifestyle*

HOOK: "GRWM while I tell you about the best [PRODUCT CATEGORY] I've ever tried"

BODY:
"[Start getting ready] Okay so I've been using [PRODUCT] for [TIMEFRAME] now and I need to talk about it.

[Continue routine, show product naturally] The reason I love this is [KEY BENEFIT]. Like look at [SPECIFIC VISUAL RESULT].

[Compare to alternatives] I used to use [OLD PRODUCT] and this is just... on another level. [SPECIFIC COMPARISON].

[Final look] And the finished result? [REACT TO HOW YOU LOOK/FEEL]"

CTA: "It's [PRICE] and honestly worth every penny. Link in bio."

## Script 7: Comparison/Review

*Best for: Competitive products, tech, software, anything with alternatives*

HOOK: "I tested [PRODUCT] vs [COMPETITOR] so you don't have to"

BODY:
"Okay let's settle this. [PRODUCT A] vs [PRODUCT B]. I bought both and used them for [TIMEFRAME].

[Show Product A] Product A: [PROS]. But also [CONS].

[Show Product B] Product B: [PROS]. But [CONS].

For [USE CASE 1]? [WINNER] wins easily.
For [USE CASE 2]? [WINNER] takes it.
For the price? [VALUE JUDGMENT].

My honest pick: [YOUR CHOICE] because [MAIN REASON]."

CTA: "I'll put links to both in the description so you can decide for yourself."

## Script 8: Street Interview Style

*Best for: Viral potential, engagement bait, brand awareness*

HOOK: "I asked strangers to try [PRODUCT] and their reactions were priceless"

BODY:

"[Approach person 1] Hey, have you ever tried [PRODUCT]?
Person: [REACTION]
[Let them try it]
Person: [GENUINE REACTION]

[Approach person 2 - different demographic]
[Same format, different reaction]

[Approach person 3 - most entertaining reaction]
[Best reaction saved for last]"

CTA: "Everyone who tried it loved it. Link in bio to try it yourself."

Note: This format works amazingly well for AI UGC too. Generate different "people" reacting to the product.

## Script 9: Tutorial/How-To

*Best for: Software, tools, apps, techniques, complex products*

HOOK: "How to [ACHIEVE RESULT] in [SHORT TIME] with [PRODUCT]"

BODY:

"Okay so this is stupid easy.

Step 1: [SIMPLE FIRST STEP]. [Show it]
Step 2: [NEXT STEP]. [Show it]
Step 3: [FINAL STEP]. [Show it]

And that's literally it. [SHOW THE RESULT]

The thing most people get wrong is [COMMON MISTAKE]. Instead, just [CORRECT APPROACH].

I use this for [YOUR USE CASE] and it saves me [TIME/MONEY/EFFORT] every single [TIME PERIOD]."

<!-- page 8 -->

CTA: "Try it yourself, link in bio. You'll thank me later."

## Script 10: Story Time

*Best for: Emotional products, premium items, brand story, anything with a narrative*

HOOK: "Story time: How [PRODUCT] literally saved my [SITUATION]"

BODY:
"Okay so this is a bit of a story but stick with me.

[TIMEFRAME] ago I was [SITUATION/CONTEXT]. Things were [EMOTIONAL STATE].

One day [HOW YOU DISCOVERED PRODUCT]. At first I thought [INITIAL REACTION].

But then [TURNING POINT MOMENT]. And I'm not being dramatic, [SPECIFIC RESULT].

[EMOTIONAL BEAT] Looking back, I wish someone had told me about this sooner because [REASON].

Now I [CURRENT STATE WITH PRODUCT]. And I tell everyone about it."

CTA: "I'm telling YOU about it now. Link in bio. You need this."
