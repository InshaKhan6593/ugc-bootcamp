## Why The Hook Is Everything

*You have 1-2 seconds before they scroll*

The hook is the single most important part of any piece of content. It doesn't matter how good your video is if nobody watches past the first second. Your hook is the bouncer at the door. It decides whether people get in or walk past.

A great hook does one of three things: creates curiosity, triggers emotion, or promises value. That's it. Every viral video you've ever watched nailed at least one of these.

Below are 100+ hooks organized by category. Steal them, adapt them, make them your own.

## Curiosity Hooks

*Make them NEED to know what happens next*

* "Nobody is talking about this and it's a problem"

* "I just found out something that changes everything"

* "This is the part they don't show you"
