## STEP 2 — GENERATE THE 5 VIDEO GENERATIONS

## PROMPT 1:

**GENERATION 1 — SCENES 1+2: REVEAL + STARTING POINT (13 sec)**

Format: 9:16. 2D ANIMATED FILM — a hand-painted animated sequence from a premium animated feature. This is explicitly NOT live-action footage, NOT a photorealistic render, NOT real human actors, NOT a 3D render. Flat animation-style rendering with painterly gouache brushwork, simplified stylized facial features and character animation typical of premium animated film. CHARACTER LINE: confident painterly outline, slightly elongated stylized proportions, simplified graphic facial features. COLOR: rich saturated warm palette (gold, ochre, terracotta) against cool accents (deep blue, muted teal), painted in visible brushstrokes, never flat or photographic. LIGHT: painted chiaroscuro, strong directional key light, deliberate rim-light, believable painted cast shadows. SURFACE: visible gouache and paper-grain texture on characters and backgrounds, film grain 3%, hand-drawn imperfection. BACKGROUND: multi-layered painterly environment, visible brushwork, atmospheric perspective, epic scale in wide shots. EXCLUDE: photorealistic, live-action, real human footage, 3D render, CGI, anime screentone, oversized anime eyes, chibi proportions, flat vector fill, glossy render, gradient banding, generic blur/cross-dissolve transitions.

Characters in this generation: @mila

BASELINE: @mila is a naturally anxious, easily overwhelmed young woman living under constant private stress — establish this clearly through her body language before anything else happens in the film.

**SCENE 1 — THE REVEAL (0:00–0:05):**

SHOT 1 (0:00–0:02): Wide city street, morning, painted illustration world. Camera: slow parallax drift moving right, following the pedestrian flow (35mm anamorphic, f/4). @mila walks mid-stride, visibly tense in her posture — shoulders slightly hunched, arms held close, the specific body language of someone braced for the next small disaster. A heavy, swollen, dark grey-black cloud sits directly attached above her head, raining steadily, visibly darker and more turbulent than the faint, calm little weathers drifting over everyone else nearby. Her hair and coat react to a light gust from her own cloud. Soft morning haze, layered watercolor sky. TRANSITION: a hard graphic cut, timed to a gust of wind from her cloud, snapping directly into a closer angle — no blur, no dissolve.

SHOT 2 (0:02–0:04): Medium shot, slow lateral tracking dolly matching her exact pace (50mm, f/2.8). Her face shows real tension — a tight jaw, eyes darting briefly to a loud car horn off-frame, a flinch quickly suppressed. Her cloud thickens for a beat in direct response, rain intensifying, then eases only slightly. TRANSITION: whip-pan following her eye-line as she glances up at her own cloud, landing directly on the close-up.

SHOT 3 (0:04– 0:05): Close-up on her face (85mm, f/1.8, shallow), looking up briefly at her own attached cloud with a small, tired, self-aware exhale — an expression of someone exhausted by a feeling she can't put down — then back to a composed public mask as she resumes walking.

SCENE TRANSITION → a sudden gust from her cloud kicks a loose leaf into frame; the leaf's motion carries directly, hard-cut, into a leaf being kicked up in the kindergarten yard at the top of the next scene — a physical object match, not a blur.

**SCENE 2 — THE STARTING POINT (0:05–0:13):**
