# STEP 1 — CREATE CHARACTER IMAGES (GPT IMAGE 2.0 or Nanobanana Pro)

*Generate one clean reference photo per character → save → upload to PromptWise as a Reference Element → tag exactly as shown in video prompts below.*

**1. @miya**

*Appears in every scene.*

photograph: a woman with shoulder-length brunette hair in a podcast studio setting

**PROMPT:**

Create a highly realistic vertical podcast studio portrait, 9:16 aspect ratio. Late 20s to early 30s, shoulder-length dark brunette wavy bob, natural soft makeup, calm thoughtful expression, sitting in a white boucle armchair during a podcast interview. She is wearing a beige knitted sleeveless crop tank top, light blue high-waisted straight-leg jeans, and layered delicate gold necklaces. She sits upright but relaxed, legs crossed, hands resting naturally in her lap, shoulders slightly angled toward the microphone, looking just past the microphone as if listening to the host.

The frame should be clean and minimal, with the woman placed slightly left of centre. A black podcast microphone with a large black foam windscreen extends from the upper right of the frame on a black adjustable boom arm. The mic should sit close to her mouth at face height, angled toward her. Add realistic metal joints, bolts, cable loops and studio hardware details. The microphone should look professional and tactile, not CGI.

Background: soft white studio wall with a very minimal corner behind her, bright and uncluttered. The white boucle chair should have a curved sculptural shape, fluffy texture and rounded arms. The scene should feel airy, modern, feminine and premium, like a wellness/business podcast studio.

Lighting: bright high-key daylight, soft even illumination, very gentle shadows under the chin and around the chair, natural skin texture, subtle shine on the face, realistic hair strands, no over-retouching. Shot like a real editorial podcast photo on a full-frame camera, 50mm lens, f/2.8, clean detail, soft depth of field, natural colour grading, whites slightly warm rather than pure sterile white.

Avoid: plastic skin, fake perfect symmetry, distorted hands, warped mic arm, overly white blown-out background, harsh studio flash, excessive blur, heavy orange tan, unrealistic jewellery, cartoon/anime style, AI glamour face.
