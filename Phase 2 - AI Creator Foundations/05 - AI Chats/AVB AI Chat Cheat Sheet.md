# AI VIDEO BOOTCAMP

## AI Chat Cheat Sheet

How to Get Better Results from Wise, ChatGPT, Claude and Other AI Chats

<!-- page 2 -->

Most people use AI chats badly. They type one vague sentence, get an average answer, and assume the tool is not that powerful.

The real skill is learning how to direct the AI. Think of it less like Google and more like a creative assistant, prompt engineer, strategist and problem-solver that can help you build better ideas before you ever spend a credit on images or video.

Use this cheat sheet whenever you are stuck, writing prompts, creating scripts, fixing bad generations, planning content, or trying to turn a rough idea into something usable.

<!-- page 3 -->

## The Simple Formula

Every strong AI chat request usually includes four things.

### 1. Context

Tell the AI what you are working on.

*"I'm creating cinematic AI videos for TikTok aimed at beginners who want to learn AI content creation."*

### 2. Goal

Tell the AI what you want the final result to achieve.

*"I want the video to feel exciting, beginner-friendly, and make people want to try the workflow themselves."*

### 3. Details

Give it the important creative direction.

*"The style should be photorealistic, cinematic, fast-paced, with a strong hook in the first three seconds."*

### 4. Output Format

Tell it exactly how you want the answer delivered.

*"Give me 5 ideas, then turn the best one into a full image prompt and a short video prompt."*

## The Master Prompt Template

Copy and paste this whenever you want a better answer from an AI chat:

*"I'm working on [describe what you are creating].*

*My goal is [describe the outcome you want].*

*The audience is [describe who it is for].*

*The style should be [describe tone, mood, visual style, pacing, realism, etc].*

*I want to avoid [describe what you do not want].*

*Help me by [describe the exact task].*

*Please format the answer as [ideas / script / prompt / table / checklist / JSON / step-by-step plan]."*

### Example

*"I'm working on a cinematic AI image for Instagram. My goal is to create a dark fantasy scene that feels like a serious film still, not a game or cartoon. The audience is people who enjoy epic fantasy visuals. The style should be photorealistic, atmospheric, dramatic and moody, with realistic lighting and natural imperfections. I want to avoid anything glossy, plastic, over-polished or obviously AI-generated. Help me develop the idea, improve the visual direction, and then write a detailed image prompt. Please format the answer with sections for subject, environment, camera, lighting, mood, realism details and negative instructions."*

## Better Than "Give Me a Prompt"

Instead of saying:

*"Give me a prompt for an image."*

Say:

*"I'm creating an AI image for [platform]. The niche is [niche]. The image should show [subject/action/location]. I want it to feel [mood/style]. Use [camera angle/lens/framing/lighting]. Make it suitable for [purpose]. Write me a detailed prompt that avoids generic AI-looking results."*

## Example

*"I'm creating an AI image for a TikTok fantasy page. The niche is dark cinematic storytelling. The image should show a lone knight standing in heavy rain outside a ruined castle. I want it to feel melancholic, powerful and realistic, like a frame from a serious medieval film. Use a low-angle camera, dramatic natural lighting, wet armour, visible texture, storm clouds and atmospheric depth. Write me a detailed image prompt that avoids glossy, plastic, game-like or cartoon-looking results."*

# Prompt Templates for Every Situation

## Prompt for Better Ideas

Use this when you are stuck and need stronger ideas.

*"Act as my creative director. I'm creating [type of content] for [platform/audience]. Give me 10 original ideas that are visually strong, easy to understand, and not generic. For each idea, include the concept, the visual hook, why it would stop people scrolling, and how I could turn it into an AI image or video."*

## Prompt for Improving a Weak Idea

Use this when your idea feels boring or too obvious.

*"Here is my idea: [paste idea].*

*Be brutally honest. What is weak, generic or predictable about it? How can we make it more original, more visual and more likely to grab attention? Give me 5 upgraded versions of the idea."*

## Prompt for Writing Image Prompts

Use this before generating images.

*"I want to create an AI image based on this idea: [paste idea].*

*Turn it into a highly detailed image prompt. Include subject, environment, composition, camera angle, lens feel, lighting, textures, mood, realism details and what to avoid. Make it feel photorealistic and natural, not overly polished or obviously AI-generated."*

## Prompt for Fixing Fake-Looking Images

Use this when your image looks too AI-generated.

*"This image/prompt is coming out too fake. The main problems are: [describe what looks wrong, plastic skin, glossy lighting, fake background, weird hands, studio lighting, blurry details, etc].*

*Here is the original prompt: [paste prompt].*

*Analyse what is causing the fake look, then rewrite the prompt to make it feel more realistic, natural and believable."*

## Prompt for Better Video Prompts

Use this before generating AI video.

*"I want to turn this image/concept into an AI video: [describe or paste concept].*

*Write a detailed video prompt with camera movement, subject movement, environmental motion, lighting changes, realism details and sound direction if needed. The video should feel like real footage, not CGI. Avoid overdramatic movement, fake slow motion, warped faces, rubbery motion or unrealistic camera behaviour."*

<!-- page 7 -->

## Prompt for Troubleshooting Bad Video Results

Use this when the video output comes out wrong.

*"I generated a video using this prompt: [paste prompt].*

*The result went wrong in these ways: [describe problems].*

*Diagnose why this happened. Tell me which parts of the prompt may have confused the model, what details are missing, and how to rewrite it for a cleaner, more realistic result."*

# More Prompt Templates

## Prompt for Scripts

Use this for TikToks, ads, lessons or voiceovers.

*"Write me a [length] script about [topic] for [audience].*
*The tone should be [tone].*
*The goal is to [educate / sell / entertain / build curiosity / explain].*
*Start with a strong hook, keep the language natural, and avoid sounding corporate, cheesy or overly hypey. Make it feel like I'm speaking directly to the viewer."*

### Example

*"Write me a 45-second TikTok script about how AI video generation works for complete beginners. The tone should be casual, exciting and easy to understand. Start with a curiosity hook in the first three seconds, explain the idea simply, and end by making people want to try the workflow themselves. Avoid sounding corporate or cringe."*

## Prompt for Critiquing Your Own Work

Use this before publishing anything.

*"Act as a brutally honest creative director. Here is my work: [paste script/prompt/idea/ad].*
*Tell me what is strong, what is weak, where people might lose interest, what feels generic, and what I should change to make it more powerful."*

## Prompt for Making Something More Natural

Use this when the answer sounds too robotic.

*"This is too polished and AI-sounding. Rewrite it so it feels more natural, human and conversational. Keep the value, but make it sound like a real person explaining it clearly. Avoid short choppy sentences, corporate wording and motivational fluff."*

## Prompt for Creating Variations

Use this when you want options.

*"Give me 10 variations of this idea. Keep the core concept the same, but change the angle, mood, setting, hook, visual style and execution. Make each version feel genuinely different, not just slightly reworded."*

## Prompt for Turning a Messy Idea into a Clear Plan

Use this when your thoughts are all over the place.

<!-- page 9 -->

*"I have a rough idea, but it is messy. Here it is: [paste idea].*
*Help me organise it into a clear creative plan. First, summarise the core idea. Then improve it. Then give me the best version of the concept, a step-by-step creation workflow, and the exact prompts I need."*

### Prompt for JSON Format

Use this when you need a structured output.

*"Now convert this into clean JSON format. Keep the meaning exactly the same, but organise it into useful fields such as subject, environment, camera, lighting, style, motion, mood, negative instructions and final prompt."*

## Follow-Up Commands That Make Any Answer Better

After the AI gives you an answer, do not stop there. Push it.

Use these follow-ups:

* "Make this more specific."

* "Make this less generic."

* "Give me 5 stronger versions."

* "Make it more cinematic."

* "Make it more realistic."

* "Make it sound more natural."

* "Make the hook stronger."

* "Cut this down by 30%."

* "Make it more beginner-friendly."

* "Make it more premium."

* "Make it less corporate."

* "Explain what is weak about this."

* "Rewrite this like I'm talking to a friend."

* "Give me the version that is most likely to stop someone scrolling."

* "Now turn this into a prompt I can actually use."

* "Now structure it into sections."

* "Now convert it into JSON."

## Common Problems and What to Ask

**Problem: The result looks fake**

*"What in this prompt is making the result look fake, glossy or AI-generated? Rewrite it to feel more natural and realistic."*

**Problem: The idea feels boring**

*"What is predictable about this idea? Give me 5 more original versions with stronger visual hooks."*

**Problem: The script sounds robotic**

*"Rewrite this so it sounds like a real person speaking naturally. Keep it useful, but remove anything corporate, cheesy or over-polished."*

**Problem: The image lacks realism**

*"What realism details are missing from this prompt? Add camera, lighting, texture, imperfections and natural environmental details."*

**Problem: The video movement looks weird**

*"What parts of this video prompt could cause unnatural motion? Rewrite it with subtle realistic camera and subject movement."*

**Problem: You do not know what to create**

*"Based on my niche, give me 20 content ideas that are visually strong, easy to make with AI, and likely to get attention on social media."*

## The Golden Rule

Do not use AI chats once and stop.

Use them as a conversation.

Your first message creates the starting point. Your follow-up messages create the quality.

The people who get the best results from AI are not always the most technical. They are the people who give clear direction, explain what they want, give feedback, and keep improving the output until it works.

Before you generate an image, talk to Wise.
Before you generate a video, talk to Wise.
Before you waste credits, talk to Wise.
Before you give up on a bad result, talk to Wise.

AI chats are where you think, plan, improve and troubleshoot.

The better you get at talking to them, the better every part of your AI creation workflow becomes.
