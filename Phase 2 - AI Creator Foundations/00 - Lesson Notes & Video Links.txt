Important note: Don't mess up your Aspect Ratios!Aspect ratio is basically the shape of your content. Choosing the right one matters because the wrong shape can make your image or video feel awkward, cropped, or less native to the platform.

### 9:16 — Vertical Full Screen
[Image 1: https://assets.skool.com/f/1a18982c15714b0c97031894dbdb3900/c583b46119014c1790e6db12802cb298aa4f8fef519b4fd996438b1540e291b6-md.png]
Use this for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, Snapchat, and mobile-first videos.
This is the main format for short-form content. It fills the phone screen, feels native, and gives you the most attention because there is no wasted space. Use 9:16 for AI videos, talking-head content, cinematic shorts, UGC ads, POV videos, character videos, and anything designed for scrolling feeds.
Best for: short-form video, mobile ads, Reels, TikToks, Shorts, POV content, cinematic vertical content.

### 4:5 — Portrait Feed Post
[Image 2: https://assets.skool.com/f/1a18982c15714b0c97031894dbdb3900/4bec685ca0714fb8aa3d18f376d697aedad8f1fbd737435f808452901ac4b029-md.png]
Use this for Instagram feed posts, Facebook feed ads, LinkedIn image posts, and high-quality social visuals.
4:5 is great because it takes up more screen space than a square post without being as tall as a Reel. It feels premium, polished, and works really well for influencer photos, product images, carousels, AI-generated lifestyle shots, and static ad creatives.
Best for: Instagram feed images, static ads, influencer photos, product visuals, carousel slides.

### 1:1 — Square
[Image 3: https://assets.skool.com/f/1a18982c15714b0c97031894dbdb3900/5feb648ff69f438cbcd4c49ea57a89f75895ae5ef46b43dc8b8c4630b506fceb.png]

Use this when you need something simple, flexible, and platform-safe (mostly for Static Ads).
Square works almost everywhere, but it is not always the most attention-grabbing format. It is useful for profile-style graphics, thumbnails, simple announcements, older ad placements, or when you need one creative that can be used across multiple platforms without much cropping.
Best for: simple graphics, profile posts, thumbnails, announcements, safe multi-platform posts.

### 16:9 — Landscape / Widescreen
[Image 4: https://assets.skool.com/f/1a18982c15714b0c97031894dbdb3900/670e764ba8c743dcafce5747fab9c8724e8ce665e0cc47e5ac1257263219bb1b-md.png]
Use this for YouTube videos, websites, presentations, cinematic scenes, and horizontal ads.
16:9 is the classic cinematic and YouTube format. It gives you more space for environments, landscapes, group shots, product scenes, tutorials, and storytelling. It is not ideal for TikTok or Reels unless you are intentionally going for a widescreen look.
Best for: YouTube, websites, trailers, cinematic videos, courses, presentations, horizontal ads.

## Simple Rule
Use 9:16 for short-form video.
Use 4:5 for Instagram/feed images and static ads.
Use 1:1 when you need something safe and simple.
Use 16:9 for YouTube, websites, courses, and cinematic horizontal content.

There is more aspect ratios, but these are the main ones you'll need.

What is a prompt?
A prompt is simply the instruction you give to AI.
It’s what you type in to tell the AI what you want it to create, answer, change, or help with.
For example:
“Create an image of a knight standing in a dark forest.”
That sentence is the prompt.
The better and clearer your prompt is, the better the AI can understand what you want.
